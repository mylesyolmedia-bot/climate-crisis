The Different Aspects of Climate Change in California

Extreme Heat

Rising temperaturs are making heat waves more frequent and severe across California, leading to greater rates of death and illness, increased expore to diseases, and worsening air and water quality. All of these combinations bring significant economic consequences for California.

Wildfires

Intense heat dries out vegetation, creating conditions that ignite and spread wildfires more rapidly. These fires cause widespread displacement, damage ecosystems, and drives major economic losses.

https://climateresilience.ca.gov/overview/impacts.html

Air Quality

The smoke produced by these fires poses serious health risks, particularly to vulnerable groups such as children, elderly people, pregnant women, and people with heart or lung conditions. These groups face a risk of harm from prolonged exposure.

https://ehs.stanford.edu/wildfire-smoke

Droughts

Droughts place a big strain on the agriculture industry in California. Although warmer climate does bring some benefits for some other regions in California for agrilcuture, those are outweighed by the disadvantage of water scarcity and invasive pests.

https://water.ca.gov/Programs/All-Programs/Climate-Change-Program/Climate-Change-and-Water

Water Supply

Droughts reshape California's water supply. Because of climate change, precipitation falls as rain rather than snow, leading to snowpack to be reduced.

https://water.ca.gov/Programs/All-Programs/Climate-Change-Program

Sea Level Rise

Rising seas level threathen the Coastline of California. Coast communities face erosion, contamination between saltwater and freshwater sources, and losses of California beaches.

https://climateresilience.ca.gov/overview/impacts.html
